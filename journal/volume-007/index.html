The Observer

Six essays on the observer rather than the observed. What familiarity removes from the person who runs a space. What the first ten minutes reveal before editorial instinct closes in. What a guest reads at the threshold that the host no longer sends consciously. What the camera's frame removes from the room it records. Why the edited image is more persuasive than the experience itself. And what the editor owes the reader who will never visit.
