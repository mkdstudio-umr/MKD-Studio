Time

Six essays on time as the design element a brief never specifies. The room that changes completely between morning and evening without a single object moving. What a season does to a building its architect photographed in summer. The space about to be dismantled that nobody thought to record. The detail that places a room in a decade without a timestamp. What a century of photographs does to the experience of walking into a building. And the room that asks you, without a word, to stay longer than you planned.
