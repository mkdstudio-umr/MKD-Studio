Without a Face

Six essays on the people the archive does photograph, and the faces it still refuses. The figure cut down to a hand, the back that never turns, the person present only as a shadow, the crowd in which no single face matters, the pilgrim erased to a silhouette, and one figure walking away, kept anonymous on purpose.
