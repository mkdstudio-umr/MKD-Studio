Publication Statement

The building is in Kensington. The work begins elsewhere.

Before installation begins, Glaze Aluminium Facades’ work on Barkers is taking shape at the fabrication facility. Brass profiles supplied by Secco Sistemi are being measured, machined and assembled into frames that will later form part of the project in London.

Thomas Hargreaves and Liam Reddington of Glaze Aluminium Facades review the project with Dave, who owns the fabrication facility, working through drawings, measurements and practical fabrication considerations together.

This first chapter records that early stage and the working relationships behind it. Those discussions connect Glaze’s coordination of the Barkers package with the work required to produce it, and carry on across the facility as the components move through production.

Across the facility, the profiles move through a clear sequence. Materials are unpacked and checked before measurements are transferred, sections are machined and individual components begin to form complete frames. Each stage contributes to the quality, accuracy and finish of what will eventually be delivered to Kensington.

The photographs document more than the appearance of the factory. They record the decisions, specialist knowledge and collaboration involved in preparing these components before they reach the site.

Before Kensington is the opening chapter of a longer photographic record. Future chapters will follow Glaze’s work through delivery, installation and completion, building a complete account of the Barkers project from its earliest fabrication stages to the finished building.
